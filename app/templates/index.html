What happens after you upload

  1. Stage 1 URL & content-hash deduplication
  2. Stage 2 fast perceptual hashing (aHash, dHash, pHash, wHash)
  3. Stage 3 candidate filtering
  4. Stage 4 pixel & structural comparison (SSIM, NRMSE, gradients, edit attribution)
  5. Stage 5 feature & embedding verification (ORB + RANSAC, template localisation)
  6. Stage 6 cross-engine merge of duplicate results

Every threshold and weight is declared in app/config.py and documented in docs/MATCHING.md. Nothing here uses a search engine’s own similarity label to decide a match.
